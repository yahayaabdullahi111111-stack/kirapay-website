About KiraPay

KiraPay is a digital communication platform being developed to help users manage their accounts, make payments, maintain wallet balances and access voice calling services.

Our goal is to provide users with a simple and convenient experience for digital communication and related account services.

Our Services

  • Account management
  • Payment and wallet features
  • Voice calling features, subject to service availability

Contact
